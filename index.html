Supplementary Materials – Master Thesis

Text Mining Reddit Narratives of Abusive Relationships:
Topics and Emotion Patterns in Online Peer Support

Anna Alina Wijsbeek · University of Twente · Master’s programme: Positive Clinical Psychology and Technology · July 2026

Overview

This website provides supplementary materials for a master thesis analysing publicly available Reddit posts from r/abusiverelationships using transformer-based text mining methods. The analysis included BERTopic topic modelling, transformer-based sentiment analysis, transformer-based emotion classification, visualisation, and statistical comparisons across topics.

The raw Reddit data are not included for ethical and privacy reasons.

Analysis Code

The analysis code is available in the repository's code folder.

  • 01_data_scraping_R.R – R script used for Reddit data extraction.
  • 02_master_thesis_analysis_code.ipynb – Main Python analysis notebook.

Ethical Data Handling

The analysis was based on publicly available Reddit posts from r/abusiverelationships. To reduce the risk of user identification, the raw Reddit data are not included in this repository.

No verbatim Reddit posts or comments are reproduced. Results are reported in aggregated form only. The repository contains only code, aggregated outputs, visualisations, model-related files, and supplementary documentation.

Ethical approval was granted by the BMS Ethical Committee / Domain Humanities & Social Sciences at the University of Twente. Application number: 260022.

Data Availability

The raw Reddit data are not publicly shared due to ethical and privacy considerations. The materials provided here are sufficient to inspect the computational workflow and aggregated results, but they do not enable reconstruction of individual Reddit posts.
