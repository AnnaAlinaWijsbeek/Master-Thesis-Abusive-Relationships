Supplementary Materials – Master Thesis

Text Mining Reddit Narratives of Abusive Relationships:
Topics and Emotion Patterns in Online Peer Support

Anna Alina Wijsbeek · University of Twente · July 2026

Overview

This repository contains supplementary materials for a master thesis analysing publicly available Reddit posts from r/abusiverelationships using BERTopic topic modelling, transformer-based sentiment analysis, emotion classification, visualisation, and statistical comparisons across topics.

Raw Reddit data are not included for ethical and privacy reasons.

Ethical Data Handling

The analysis was based on publicly available Reddit posts. No verbatim Reddit posts or comments are reproduced. Results are reported in aggregated form only. Raw Reddit data are not publicly shared to reduce the risk of user identification.

Ethical approval was granted by the BMS Ethical Committee / Domain Humanities & Social Sciences at the University of Twente. Application number: 260022.
